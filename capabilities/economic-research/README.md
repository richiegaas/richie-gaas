Interceptor loadout allocation research for Guam missile defense — `spec.md` defines the constrained-optimization model, interceptor input table, raid scenarios, and required figures; `model.xlsx` is the Excel and Solver build (Table 1 inputs, 16 sites, up to 8 Patriot batteries at 2 sites each, and the three Table 2 options; its `Table 2` sheet shows where each number in the paper's Table 2 comes from), with every input still a draft until confirmed against a primary source; `reference-optimizer.py` is the independent check behind its reference results. The paper's drafts are in `drafts/`; the brief is `docs/briefs/research-brief.md`.
