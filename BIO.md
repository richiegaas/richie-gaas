
# Richelbert (Richie) Gaas

### Richie Gaas is a West Point graduate and military leader transitioning into business and entrepreneurship, with interests spanning defense, artificial intelligence, strategy, and emerging technology. Over more than a decade of service across the United States and Indo-Pacific, he has guided teams through complex environments while developing strengths in strategic planning, organizational management, problem-solving, and people development.

### Richie holds a B.S. in International Relations from the United States Military Academy and is currently pursuing an MBA. His background in air, missile, and space operations has shaped a broader curiosity about how new technologies, thoughtful decision-making, and strong organizations can address difficult challenges.

### As he begins his next chapter, Richie is eager to connect with mentors and collaborators across consulting, national security, innovation, and enterprise development. He hopes to contribute a practical perspective shaped by service while continuing to learn from others with diverse expertise. Ultimately, Richie plans to build a technology solutions company centered on advisory services, executive coaching, education, and tools that help people and organizations perform more effectively.
